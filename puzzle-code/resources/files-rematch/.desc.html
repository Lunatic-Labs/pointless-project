%TITLE Plot Coupons

Decrypted, and ahead of schedule!

Dr. Pepper hands over the code itself — the actual notes, in his actual handwriting. "It may help you on your quest," he says, which is what everyone here says.

Every quest has this part: three trials, three fragments, one artifact. Scholars call the fragments Plot Coupons. Collect the full set, mail them in, receive one (1) plot.

Here you will replay three puzzles you have already solved, but a bit remixed. Each time you complete one, you will be given a number.

When you have all three numbers, put them together in order (rematch 1, then 2, then 3) to forge the master key.

The master key opens exactly one thing, once. Standard, for master keys in this line of work.
