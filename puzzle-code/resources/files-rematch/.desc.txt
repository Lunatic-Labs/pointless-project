<p>With "Dr. Pepper's code" and 0 extra points, you have made it to the round of rematches!</p>

<p>Here you will replay three puzzles you have already solved, but a bit remixed. Each time you complete one, you will be given a number. Those numbers are the <b>key</b> to your success.</p>

<p>When you have all three numbers, put them together in order (rematch 1, then 2, then 3) to get the password.</p>
