With "Dr. Pepper's code," and 0 points extra, you have made it to the round of rematches! In here you will complete three puzzles that you have already completed, but a bit remixed. Each time you complete a puzzle, you will be given a number. These numbers will be important to finish the rematch as it will be the <u><b>key</b></u> to your success. When you have all three numbers, put them together in order (rematch 1, then 2, then 3) to get the password.
