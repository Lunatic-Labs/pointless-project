%TITLE Mysterious Artifact

Good job encoding those instructions! You find a map of somewhere else and decide to keep it in your inventory.

A mysterious artifact with blinking lights appears. By a sudden fit of intuition you realize exactly one light is lit in each column. A light on the bottom is red and means that column contributes nothing, but the other green lights represent a value, counting up from the bottom. For example, the light in the first column below means 2.

You suddenly decide to call the total number of lights (red plus green) in column the base. The lights in the second column represent multiples of the base, going up. For example, the second column below means 12.

The third column means zero, but if the bottom-most green light were on, it would mean 16.

So, the total value represented by the artifact below is 2 + 12 + 0 = 14.
